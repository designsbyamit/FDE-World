Problem Statement

Bayer's cash flow is constrained by delayed distributor and pharmacy payments, resulting in extended DSO and manual follow-up processes. Collections analysts operate across multiple disconnected systems (SAP PCM, Outlook, Salesforce), spending excessive time identifying risk, drafting emails, and escalating disputes.

Business Opportunity:

An AI powered Collections assistant built on SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) can automate risk detection, streamline collections workflows, and provide explainable, AI-driven insights. By combining structured financials with unstructured communication signals, the system proactively identifies high-risk accounts, drafts next-best actions, and reduces manual work while maintaining human control.

User Scenarios:

  • Collection team manage overdue accounts within a Taskboard.
  • AI assists by predicting late payments, prioritizing accounts, drafting communication, and triggering workflow drafts for disputes.
  • Executives need visibility into payment risk, sentiment, and DSO trends across regions.
Current State Pain Points
  • Risk scoring is limited to structured AR data, missing behavioral or sentiment indicators from communications and contracts.
  • Analysts manually create workflows and correspondence, consuming up to XX% of effort.
  • Executives lack a unified, explainable view of payment risks, customer sentiment, and DSO trends.
  • Collections analysts work across multiple disconnected systems (SAP PCM, Outlook, Salesforce), spending excessive time identifying risk, drafting emails, and escalating disputes.
Objectives

Expected Outcomes:

  • Reduce DSO by 2–4 days via early risk prediction and proactive actioning
  • Increase analyst productivity by 70% through automation and AI-assisted drafting
  • Improve forecast accuracy by 15% via explainable AI-driven dashboards

Key Features:

  • Risk Taskboard: Prioritized accounts with explainable AI risk drivers.
  • Agentic Workflow Automation: Auto-generates SAP PCM/FSCM workflows pending analyst approval.
  • Tone-Aware Communication Drafts: Generates compliant, personalized outreach emails directly from the app.
  • Personalized Agent: Context-aware interaction layer for simulation, reasoning, and action execution.
  • Executive Dashboard: Portfolio risk, DSO, and AI explainability metrics for leadership.

Optimization and Simulation Capabilities:

  • Pricing optimization (discount or penalty for best financial outcome)
  • Incentive elasticity modeling
  • Discount sensitivity analysis
  • Contract simulation / revenue uplift modeling
  • Forecasting impact of a penalty on DSO

Credit and Collections Manager Recommendation Cockpit:

  • Recommend top N actions with measurable impact on working capital / DSO (e.g. adjust payment terms, unblock orders, extend credit limits)
  • Proactive simulation capabilities supporting what-if analysis on credit limits, payment terms, order block releases, early payment incentives, and interest fees

Constraints

Out of Scope:

  • Legacy non-SAP financial systems
  • Fully autonomous collections without human validation
  • Third-party CRM plug-ins
  • Regional deployments restricted by legal or compliance constraints

Architecture:

  • Front-end: SAP BTP
  • Core AI: Hybrid Risk Model + RAG Orchestration Layer
  • Back-end: SAP FSCM, Bayer Data Lake, Vector DB, and AI Core Integration
  • Governance: Built-in auditability, bias evaluation, and SAP AI Ethics compliance

Deployment:

  • Environment: SAP BTP (cloud-native)
  • CI/CD Flow: GitHub Actions integrated with SAP AI Core pipelines

Scope for Design
Collections analyst triages overdue accounts
Analyst opens the AI Taskboard and reviews customer accounts flagged as High, Medium, or Low Risk with color-coded accents. They select a high-risk account and see the top 3 risk drivers (e.g. declining payment behavior, dispute history) with a confidence indicator to guide their next action.
Collections analyst asks the agent for insights
Analyst types a natural-language request such as "Show overdue payments for A Corp" into the conversational agent. The agent responds with actionable proposals like "Simulate Impact" or "Generate Email," and the analyst chooses how to proceed.
Collections analyst reviews and approves an AI-drafted email
Analyst opens an AI-generated, tone-aware outreach email tailored to the customer, edits if needed, and sends it directly from the app without switching to Outlook.
Collections manager walks an agentic workflow to a decision
Manager opens a flagged account (e.g. "Zurich Industrial") and steps through the Propose → Approve → Create FSCM Case workflow. They review the explainable risk panel showing the top drivers, approve the AI's proposed action, and the FSCM case is created.
Collections manager runs a what-if simulation
Manager selects a customer and adjusts inputs such as discount percentage, penalty percentage, and elasticity type using sliders and dropdowns. They see the predicted impact on DSO, working capital, and cash flow, and export the scenario snapshot for review.
Collections manager reviews top recommended actions
Manager opens the Recommendation Cockpit and sees the top N actions ranked by measurable impact (e.g. adjust payment terms, extend credit limit, don't unblock a specific order). They drill into a recommendation to see expected impact on KPIs and decide whether to act.
Executive monitors portfolio risk and DSO trends
Executive opens the dashboard summarizing DSO, risk level, and cash flow impact across regions. They use the conversational bar to test scenarios like "Simulate 3% discount impact" and see updated KPI projections.
