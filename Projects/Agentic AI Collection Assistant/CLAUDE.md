# Agentic AI Collection Assistant

## Project Overview
A prototype demonstrating an AI agent that assists with accounts receivable collections — prioritizing overdue invoices, suggesting outreach actions, and surfacing risk signals.

## Pages
- `worklist.html` — Collections worklist showing customers with overdue balances
- `detail.html` — Detail page for a single customer/collection case
- `admin.html` — Admin/configuration view
- `screenflow.html` — Screen flow covering Flow 1 (agent experience) and Flow 2 (admin experience)

## Persona
- Reviewer is the collections agent/manager. Check existing pages for avatar initials in use.
