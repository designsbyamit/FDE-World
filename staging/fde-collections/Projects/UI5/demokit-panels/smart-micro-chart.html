Smart Micro Chart
A container that auto-selects the right micro chart type based on OData chart annotations — Bullet, Area, Line, Donut, Pie, BarStacked, Column. Each instance shows a compact SVG chart alongside its OData metadata associations (Title, Description, Unit of Measure). Equivalent to sap.ui.comp.smartmicrochart.SmartMicroChart.
