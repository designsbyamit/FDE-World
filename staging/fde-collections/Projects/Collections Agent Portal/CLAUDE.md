# Collections Agent Portal

## Purpose
A self-contained product knowledge hub and CXO story for the Collections Agent project. It is the single entry point for domain learning, product thinking, AI guardrails, prototype history, and product evidence.

## Files
- `index.html` — semantic portal structure and content
- `styles.css` — responsive editorial visual system
- `app.js` — navigation, source-backed data, progressive disclosure, and accessibility
- `assets/posters/` — local copies of the uploaded Agent UX reference posters

## Product rules
- Explain unfamiliar finance and AI concepts in plain language before adding depth.
- Label knowledge as Current, Proposed, Exploratory, Draft, Deprecated, or Archived.
- Label outcomes as Measured, Target, Estimated, or Illustrative.
- Do not claim customer outcomes without evidence.
- Keep the top level to Understand Collections, Product, Principles & Guardrails, Prototypes & Releases, Document Repository, and Experience the Story.
- No global search until the information model has stabilised.
