Agentic Experience Assessment Reference

Use after designing a flow and before usability testing or release. Record evidence for every checked item; resolve all must-have gaps or document an accepted exception.

Prototype / flow:Reviewer:Review date:Decision / follow-up owner:

1. Intent, scope, and boundaries

The user can identify the agent’s goal, task, and expected outcome.Use plain language; do not make people infer intent.
Every action is labelled read-only or write, with data and system scope.Set expectations before execution.
Out-of-scope requests receive an honest refusal and nearest supported next step.
Permissions, policy constraints, and sensitive-data boundaries are enforced.Must have

2. Human control and consent

Consequential writes require non-bypassable approval with action, records, impact, Confirm, and Cancel.Must have
Low-stakes acknowledgements use lightweight inline confirmation.
Users can override recommendations; the flow honours and records their choice.
Long-running work supports pause, cancel, and resume from a clear checkpoint.Must have
Delegated work is visible as a subtask and can be cancelled without losing parent context.

3. Trust and transparency

Recommendations show a concise “Why this?” explanation and decision factors.
AI insights show confidence; low confidence changes the interaction with a caution.
Generated insights identify source data and last-updated time.Must have
Users can inspect what ran, what was read, and what was written.Must have

4. Execution, feedback, and recovery

Progress distinguishes waiting, running, completed, and paused states.
Timeouts explain the delay and offer cancel or safe background continuation.
Partial results preserve completed work, identify failure, and support retry from that step.Must have
Data-source failures identify the source and time, offer retry, and keep unaffected content available.Must have

5. Quality gate

Test success, refusal, approval, override, pause/resume, timeout, partial-result, and source-failure paths.Must have
Controls and status changes are keyboard accessible and understandable without colour alone.Must have
Copy is specific, calm, and does not overstate certainty, authority, or automation.
A named owner accepts open risks, policy exceptions, and follow-up actions.Must have
Save the completed assessment with the release decision for later review.

6. EU AI Act evidence beyond the prototype

Classify the intended use and risk profile with legal, risk, and product owners.Must have before any compliance claim
Provide evidence for data governance, quality, bias evaluation, and privacy controls.
Provide technical documentation, traceable logs, cybersecurity, robustness, and accuracy testing evidence.
Define post-market monitoring, incident handling, human-oversight procedures, and accountable owners.

Derived from Joule Work Agent UX Patterns: Human-in-the-Loop, Trust & Transparency, and execution-recovery patterns.
