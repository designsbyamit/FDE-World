Overflow Toolbar
A toolbar that moves controls into an overflow popover when space is limited. Supports mixed control types — buttons, inputs, selects, date pickers, segmented buttons, separators, and spacers. Equivalent to sap.m.OverflowToolbar — rendered using ui5-toolbar with overflow.
