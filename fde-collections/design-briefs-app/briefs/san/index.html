Problem Statement

Sanofi's supplier master data maintenance process requires manual effort, lacks scalable automation, and needs stronger compliance, auditability, and traceability. Sanofi and SAP will jointly build an AI-driven Supplier Maintenance FDE VMD Agent on SAP BTP to automate supplier maintenance requests originating in Elementum, while preparing for future MDG 2025 integration.

Current State Pain Points
  • Manual effort in supplier maintenance workflows
  • Slow processing of supplier changes
  • Limited compliance, auditability, and traceability
  • Lack of readiness for future autonomous supplier maintenance workflows
Objectives

Business Objectives:

  • Reduce manual effort in supplier maintenance workflows
  • Accelerate supplier change processing
  • Improve compliance, auditability, and traceability
  • Prepare for future autonomous supplier maintenance workflows

Technical Objectives:

  • Build SAP-hosted supplier maintenance agent orchestration on BTP
  • Classify requests using Sanofi-defined deterministic risk rules
  • Validate required documents using OCR
  • Route approvals based on risk level and configured rules
  • Maintain full audit trail, agent history, and decision logs
  • Sync final status and decisions back to Elementum
  • Prepare for future MDG 2025 writeback readiness

Constraints
  • Supplier experience must remain inside Elementum (Sanofi's third-party vendor portal)
  • Approval, audit, and agent workflow governance must remain inside SAP
  • Document upload must be enforced upfront in Elementum for sensitive changes
  • Every action must be traceable for SOX alignment
  • What is built in the SAP landscape will be productized; customers connect via APIs or Agents
  • Risk classification must follow Sanofi-defined deterministic rules

Out of Scope – Phase 1:

  • Fully autonomous production writeback for high-risk requests
  • Production-grade Trustpair rollout
  • Advanced analytics and reporting
  • Full exception automation coverage
  • Supplier-facing SAP UI

Scope for Design
Supplier submits a maintenance request
Supplier logs into Elementum and submits a supplier maintenance request, uploading any required documents upfront when the change is sensitive. The supplier sees confirmation that their request has been submitted for processing.
Supplier receives final status
Supplier is notified in Elementum of the final outcome of their request, seeing a status such as Approved, Rejected, Escalated, or Completed without needing to leave the portal.
Approver reviews a Medium or High-risk request
Approver receives a notification and opens the SAP-hosted approval experience, where they review request details, uploaded documents, OCR results, the AI summary, and the agent run and decision history. They then approve or reject the request, with the decision syncing back to Elementum.
Internal user monitors the worklist
Sanofi internal user opens the SAP-hosted worklist and admin dashboard to view in-progress requests, track approval status, and monitor exceptions and escalations. They drill into individual requests to review agent run history and audit logs.
Admin configures risk classification rules
Admin opens the admin tab in the SAP dashboard and updates the deterministic rules that determine Low, Medium, and High risk routing for supplier field changes. The admin saves the updated configuration so future requests are classified accordingly.
Auditor reviews decision history
Internal compliance or audit user opens the dashboard to review the full audit trail of a request, including timestamped agent actions, risk classification history, OCR validation history, and approval or rejection records retained for SOX alignment.
